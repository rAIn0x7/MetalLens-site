About MetalLens

The intelligence platform focused on precious metals and commodities markets.

What We Do

MetalLens aggregates precious metals and commodities market signals and uses AI to surface the most important developments daily. Every 2 hours, our pipeline fetches new content, scores it on a 1–10 importance scale using Gemini AI, and surfaces only the stories that matter (score ≥ 7).

We don't just aggregate — we filter. The average score across all fetched content is around 5. You only see what scored 7 or above. Editor's Take provides context on why a story matters, not just what it says.

The Commodities × AI Angle

Most financial media covers equities. Most commodities media covers futures prices. Nobody covers the intersection: AI-driven commodity trading signals, geopolitical impacts on precious metals, macro trends affecting gold and silver, supply chain disruptions. That's our focus.

Scoring Dimensions

Each article is scored on: source authority, commodity relevance (gold/silver weighted higher), price/regulatory/geopolitical impact, and macro crossover relevance (bonus points). Articles scoring ≥ 9 are marked Pro — they contain the highest-signal intelligence.
